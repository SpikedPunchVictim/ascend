# EV-60: do repo-relative size and churn thresholds predict a fix on nx?

**Status**      Measured 2026-10-13 for `asc-tb92`. Predictions, thresholds and the bar were in the
                bead's description before any blame trace was run; not sealed by sha256. Nothing
                below was added after reading the result.

**Question**    `EV-59` found that n8n's rule fails on nest as written and that size carries over
                only as a threshold relative to the repository, a reading made after the fact.
                Registered first this time, on a third repository: do relative size and relative
                churn predict a later fix?

## Method

`nrwl/nx` at `994ecba49cec06db7475015344b9d175af97db66`, cloned for this record, read-only
(`git log`, `git diff`, `git blame`). Chosen by an API scan of 245 public repositories for commit
volume, typed-commit share, squash merging and low dependency noise. Outcome and fix definition as
`EV-57`: 5,737 `fix` commits traced, 19,882 traced rows, 10,049 eligible commits.

Seen before predicting, counts only: commits and typed share, the fix count, eligible commits by
year (that pre-count read 10,037; the feature script's own filter gives 10,049), and agent-marked
commits.

The five `EV-58` features at n8n's fixed thresholds, plus two relative ones whose thresholds come
from 2022 to 2025 and are applied unchanged to 2026:

- `size_top_decile`: added source lines above the 90th percentile (229 here). Stratified by type.
- `churn_top_decile`: the commit's busiest source file, by changes in the prior 30 days, is above
  the 90th percentile of that number (6 here). Stratified by size tercile and type.

**The bar:** stratified risk ratio at least 1.3 over all commits, with 5% prevalence and 20 exposed,
and at least 1.3 in 2026. The agent arm (119 commits) is reported and is not in the bar.

## Predictions

P1 `size_top_decile` clears. P2 `churn_top_decile` clears. P3 fixed `large` is gradeable and
reaches 1.3. P4 fixed `hot_file` reaches 1.3. P5 `cross_package` does not reach 1.3. P6
`no_test_alongside` lies between 0.7 and 1.3. P7 `first_touch` is below 1.0. P8 the fixed rate
rises with the count of the two relative features and is at least 3 times higher at 2 than at 0.
P9 the base fixed rate is between 10% and 20%.

## Decision

**Relative size and relative churn predict a later fix on a third repository, with the thresholds
and the bar named first.** Across the three:

| Feature             | n8n (`EV-58`) | nest (`EV-59`)                    | nx   |
| ------------------- | ------------- | --------------------------------- | ---- |
| size                | 2.58          | 2.40 ungraded; 1.95 top decile    | 2.67 |
| churn (hot file)    | 1.78          | 1.49, unstable                    | 1.72 |
| first touch         | 0.57          | 0.89                              | 0.64 |
| no test alongside   | 0.86          | 1.70                              | 0.91 |
| cross-package       | 1.50          | 0.89                              | 1.31 |

1. **Size and churn are the rule.** Both clear here and in 2026, with intervals well clear of 1.
2. **`first_touch` is inverse in all three.** Commits by an author new to those files are fixed
   less often. Not a nudge, and worth knowing: familiarity is not the protective factor.
3. **`no_test_alongside` and `cross_package` are dropped.** Each fails in two repositories of three.
4. **The count is a narrow, sharp flag**: 17% of commits carry one or both and hold about 37% of
   the fixed ones; 1.4% carry both and more than half of those are fixed.
5. **Agent-marked commits agree on size (2.25, 1.08 to 4.71) and say nothing yet about churn**
   (8 exposed commits).

Still untested, unchanged since `EV-58`: churn may partly restate the outcome, since a busy file
has more later commits to rewrite its lines; and nothing here shows that telling a session changes
what happens.

For the handler: two numbers, both read from the local repository's own last months of history by
a script, since events carry no sizes: the 90th percentile of added lines per commit, and of
30-day changes to a file.

## Confidence

**High** for the counts. **Medium to high** that size and churn predict the line-traced outcome in
conventional-commit TypeScript monorepos: three repositories, one of them weak, the last with
everything registered first. **Low** that a nudge prevents anything. Blame's limits from `EV-57`
apply; all three repositories label fixes by commit type.
