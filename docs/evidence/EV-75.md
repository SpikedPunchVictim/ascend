# EV-75: is the public-repo corpus fit to mine for betterment priors?

**Status**      Measured 2026-10-21 for `asc-m3sb.1`, the gate `asc-m3sb` section 6 put in front of
                every prior: four checks that must pass before a number from
                `~/temp/enterprise-apps/pr-research` is admitted. Two of the four changed the
                design's inputs — the Dataset B denominator, and the set of repos a PR-linked
                finding may be pooled from. Nothing was pre-registered: section 6 is a checklist of
                things to verify, not a set of predictions, so there is no prediction here to have
                been wrong about.

**Question**    Before treating a 288k-commit corpus and 3,000 review comments as a source of
                betterment priors: does the corpus say what its own README says it says, can a
                finding be linked to a PR at a rate the design's gate allows, is the reviewer
                population what it claims to be, and does the design's own outcome measure point the
                same way on our repositories as on theirs?

## Method

Read-only over `~/temp/enterprise-apps/pr-research` and over `git log` in each clone. No network, no
build, nothing written into any clone. Two scripts, both kept:

- `spike/m3sb/verify-corpus.mjs` — checks 1-3, from the corpus files alone.
- `spike/m3sb/touched-again.mjs` — check 4, one `git log --numstat` pass per repository.

The outcome in check 4 is the design's, verbatim: *"**touched_again** — L touches at least one path C
touched, within **14 days**"*, and its typed sibling *"**fix_after** — L is typed fix and touches a
path C touched, within **30 days**"*. Three things the design leaves open are fixed here, and applied
identically to both sides of the comparison:

- **Source files only**, with `EV-57`'s non-source list: locks, changelogs, README and LICENSE,
  `.md`/`.jsonl`/`.csv`/`.tsv`/`.log`, `docs/`, `examples/`, and the store's own `.ascend/` and
  `.beads/`. A re-touched lockfile is not a re-touched change.
- **30 files or fewer** per commit, `EV-56`/`EV-62`'s rule.
- **Commits inside the last window before the newest commit are dropped**, not counted as
  not-touched. They have not had 14 (or 30) days to be followed up on, and counting them would bias
  the rate down by however much the history runs up to today.

The feature compared is `size_top_decile` — a commit whose added source lines are at or above the
repository's own 90th percentile — because that is the class `EV-60`, `EV-62` and `EV-63` measured. A
group under `MIN_N` = 20 commits is reported as an anecdote, not as a rate.

**One record for four numbers.** The bead asks for an evidence record "for each number"; the four
numbers are one gate read in one sitting by one script pair, so they are recorded together rather
than split into four records that would each have to restate the method.

**The measurement moves with this repository.** ascend's own history is an input to check 4, and it
grows every time a commit lands: the run below was taken at HEAD `31902ad`, and its censored count
moved from 74 to 75 between two runs. The rates did not move.

### Check 2 — the `(#N)` link rate per repo, which is section 3's gate

```
check 2  PR-linked 82696 of 288197 = 28.69%
         poolable      cdk8s, opentelemetry-js
         per-repo only directus, langchainjs, n8n, pulumi
         not pooled    backstage, nest, strapi, vscode
```

The pooled 28.69 % is the one number nobody should use: it averages repos whose rates run from
0.08 % to 97.70 %.

**Only two repos clear the design's 0.80 pooling bar**, both small (cdk8s 2,565 subjects,
opentelemetry-js 3,222). The repo `EV-56`, `EV-57` and `EV-62` mine — n8n — lands at 0.729, in the
per-repo band, so a PR-linked finding from n8n is reported with its rate beside it and never pooled.

The `(#N)` anchor does not carry this result: matching the pattern anywhere in the subject rather
than at its end moves three repos by 0.1 points (langchainjs 71.9 → 72.0, n8n 72.9 → 73.0, vscode
27.0 → 27.1) and moves no repo across a band boundary.

**38.07 %** reproduces the README's ~38 %. It is a mean over repos whose bot shares run from **0.0 %**

Two details worth keeping. The `[bot]` login suffix identifies only **421** of the 1,142 bot rows —

the classifier and the suffix is not a substitute for it. And two nest rows have `type: null` (one
also `user: null`); they are counted and named, never folded into the human column.

**One of our three repositories can answer the question, and it says yes.** ascend's size top decile
is followed up 1.146 times as often as the rest (n = 25); n8n's is 1.313 under the same definition
(1.632 typed). mast and spindle cannot answer: after the censoring rule their top deciles hold 17 and
5 commits under `touched_again`, and 16 and 0 under `fix_after`, every one of them under `MIN_N`.
ascend's `fix_after` top decile is 12 commits and its rate is 0 — it writes almost no conventional
`fix` types (`EV-63` counted 0), which is why the typed outcome cannot be read on it.

The reason mast and spindle cannot answer is a property of our histories, not of the method: mast has
220 commits censored out of 550, and spindle 163 out of 220 under the 30-day window. Our repositories
are short and bursty, so a 14- or 30-day outcome window consumes a large share of them. The censoring
rule is what turns that into "cannot say" rather than a biased "no".

## Decision

Three numbers are now the design's, and one question is answered as far as our histories allow.

1. **Dataset B is 288,197 subjects, a snapshot ending mid-May 2026.** The README's two counts are both
   wrong, and the snapshot date is unrecorded in the corpus. Any rate that matters is recomputed from
   the files. A rate that has to track the clones is recomputed from `git log`, not from Dataset B.
2. **A PR-linked finding may be pooled from cdk8s and opentelemetry-js only.** Those are the two
   above the design's 0.80 bar. directus, langchainjs, n8n and pulumi are reported per repo with the
   rate printed beside every row. backstage, nest, strapi and vscode are not pooled at all. The
   28.69 % corpus average is used nowhere.
3. **Dataset A is reported split by repo.** 38.07 % is the pool mean; the per-repo range is
   0.0 %-79.7 %, and a bot comment is a different reviewer with a different false-positive profile.
4. **`size_top_decile` transfers in direction, on one repo's worth of evidence.** ascend agrees
   (rr 1.146, n = 25) and n8n is the reference (1.313; 1.632 typed). The class is admissible
   **provisionally**: the direction is the same where it can be measured here, and `EV-63`'s
   line-traced outcome — which is not saturated the way path overlap is — found the same direction on
   all four of our repositories (pooled rr 1.601). The caveat is that our side is one repository at
   n = 25, so a class whose margin is small must not be admitted on this alone.

## Confidence

High on checks 1-3: they are counts over files, reproducible byte for byte, and every disagreement
with the README is arithmetic rather than judgement.

Moderate on check 4's "agrees": the direction is consistent across two definitions and two pieces of
evidence (this record and `EV-63`), but our side of the comparison rests on one repository with 25
exposed commits — small, and clearing `MIN_N` by five.

## Limitations

- **The `theme-distribution.json` per-theme bot shares were not re-derived.** This record verifies the
  bot/human split, not the 14-theme bucketing's own `bot_share_pct`, because no theme classifier
  ships with the corpus. Those per-theme numbers remain as the previous study reported them.
- **`touched_again` is saturated on our side** (base rates 0.66-0.85 at 14 days), which compresses
  every ratio toward 1. That is the fault `EV-57` recorded on n8n and why a line-traced outcome
  exists: a ratio of 1.15 in a saturated measure is not comparable to 1.15 in a rare one.
- **The snapshot cutoff is measured exactly on n8n only.** For the other nine repos the evidence is
  that the file is smaller than the clone, not a dated boundary.
- **PR ↔ issue remains not measured**, as the design says: this corpus holds neither PR bodies nor
  issue records, and nothing here changes that.
- **`grizzly-pl`, one of `EV-63`'s four repositories, is no longer on this machine**, so check 4 runs
  on three of our repositories rather than four.
- **The `(#N)` rate is measured over subjects, not PRs.** A subject carrying `(#1234)` is evidence
  that a PR exists and is linked; it is not evidence that the PR's body, review or issue trail is in
  the corpus, and none of them is.
