# EV-63: do size and churn predict rework in our own repositories?

**Status**      Measured 2026-10-13 for `asc-09wx.1`. Design, bar and predictions were in the bead's
                description before any trace ran; not sealed by sha256. Nothing was added
                afterwards.

**Question**    `EV-60` confirmed repo-relative size and churn on public repositories, and `EV-62`
                found an outcome that needs no commit labels. Together they allow the test `EV-61`
                could not make: do the two features predict anything in the repositories we work in?

## Method

Read-only (`git log`, `git diff`, `git blame -w`) on the history reachable from HEAD of four
repositories: ascend (367 non-merge commits), grizzly-pl (2,118), mast (387) and spindle (220).
The other grizzly clones share grizzly-pl's root commit and are left out. Every non-merge commit
with 30 files or fewer was traced.

- **Non-source**, beyond `EV-57`'s list: `.jsonl`, `.csv`, `.tsv`, `.log`, and anything under
  `.ascend/` or `.beads/`.
- **Outcomes.** `reworked_7` (primary) and `reworked_30`, as `EV-62`: a later commit rewrites one of
  the commit's source lines inside the window. `heavily_reworked_7`: at least 25% of its added
  source lines rewritten within 7 days.
- **Features.** `size_top_decile` and `churn_top_decile`, thresholds the 90th percentile over each
  repository's own eligible commits, in-sample because the histories are too short to split.
  `no_test_alongside` is reported; `first_touch` is dropped, these repositories having one author.
- Size is a plain risk ratio; churn and no-test are stratified by size tercile; pooled ratios are
  Mantel-Haenszel across repositories.

**The bar:** pooled ratio at least 1.3 with a 95% interval clear of 1 under `reworked_7`, and at
least 1.3 in two or more repositories with 20 or more exposed commits.

**A correction to `EV-61`**, found while counting: it said none of these repositories types its
commits. mast types 340 of 387 and spindle 164 of 220. `EV-61` is corrected in place.

## Predictions

P1 `reworked_7` is above 50% in ascend and grizzly-pl. P2 size does not clear the bar: the outcome
is near its ceiling. P3 churn does not clear it either. P4 under `heavily_reworked_7`, churn
reaches 1.3 pooled and size is below 1.0. P5 `no_test_alongside` stays between 0.7 and 1.3 pooled.
P6 on mast, kappa between `reworked_7` and typed fixes is under 0.4.

## Decision

**Size predicts rework in our own repositories. Churn does not clear the bar.**

1. **A top-decile commit is reworked within a week 82% of the time, against 51% for the rest.**
   The ratio is smaller than on the public repositories because the floor is so high: half of
   everything is touched again within seven days.
2. **Rework here is mostly iteration, not correction.** A base rate of 31% to 72% says a later
   rewrite is the normal fate of a line in these repositories. The outcome ranks commits; it does
   not say the reworked ones were wrong. mast is the one place both outcomes exist, and there they
   agree at 0.515.
3. **Churn is weak here**: 1.24 pooled, 0.98 in mast. Where files change dozens of times a month,
   "busier than usual" separates little.
4. **`no_test_alongside` points the other way in the two untyped repositories**: commits with no
   test file are reworked about half as often. Those are likely the small configuration and script
   commits. It remains a feature that gives a different answer in every repository.
5. **Heavy rework is rare**: 0.7% to 10.8% of commits have a quarter of their lines rewritten in a
   week, too few to grade anything.

For the build: a size flag is supported in our own work as well as in three public repositories.
A churn flag is not, and with `EV-61` (it fires on a fifth of commits) there is no case for
shipping it.

## Confidence

**High** for the counts. **Medium** that size predicts rework here: four repositories, two with 20
or more exposed commits, thresholds in-sample, one author. **Low** that rework measures anything a
nudge should prevent; that needs an outcome closer to a defect, or the holdout.
