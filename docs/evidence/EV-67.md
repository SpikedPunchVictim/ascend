# EV-67: do deletion-heavy commits get reworked more in our own repositories?

**Status** Predictions registered 2026-10-13 in the notes of `asc-09wx.16`, before the script was
run on any of our repositories. Measured the same day.

**Question** In nx and n8n the tenth of commits that remove the most source lines were fixed more
often than commits of the same added size (1.44 and 1.48 with later file activity held fixed,
EV-66). Does the same hold in ascend, grizzly-pl, mast and spindle, where the outcome has to be
rework because most commits carry no type?

## Method (fixed before measuring)

- EV-63's saved logs and line traces for the four repositories, unchanged.
- **Heavy deletion:** a commit in its repository's top tenth by source lines removed.
- **Outcome:** `reworked_7`, any later commit rewriting one of the commit's lines within 7 days.
- **Statistic:** Mantel-Haenszel risk ratio, strata repository by size decile of added lines.
- **Bar** (EV-63's): pooled ratio at least 1.3 with the interval above 1, and at least 1.3 in two
  repositories with 20 or more exposed commits.

**Rule.** Bar cleared: removed lines join the size check. Not cleared: the recorder stores removed
lines but nothing flags on them.

A second question, whether first touch stops being protective inside the top size decile, was
dropped before measuring: these repositories have one author and the saved logs carry no author.

## Predictions

- **P1.** The bar is not cleared: the pooled ratio is between 1.0 and 1.3, because base rates this
  high leave little room.
- **P2.** The ratio is above 1 in at least three of the four repositories.
- **P3.** The pooled ratio for `heavily_reworked_7` (a quarter or more of the commit's lines
  rewritten) is larger than for `reworked_7`.
- **P4.** In mast, against typed fixes within 30 days, the ratio is at least 1.3.

## Decision

**Not cleared, so by the rule nothing flags on removed lines here.** The recorder should still
store them: the raw gap is large (72.2% of heavy-deletion commits reworked against 51.9%), and it
is size that explains it. Once commits are compared within the same added-size decile the gap is
1.08 and its interval includes 1.

The signal held in two public repositories against labelled fixes and does not hold in ours against
rework. Those are different outcomes, so this is not a contradiction of EV-66; it says the signal
does not transfer to the only outcome we can measure on our own code.

## Confidence

**Medium** that there is no effect of the size seen in nx and n8n: 108 exposed commits pooled give
an interval that excludes 1.3. **Low** for any single repository; only grizzly-pl (58) and ascend
(23) reach 20 exposed, and mast and spindle are anecdotes. Half or more of all commits here are
reworked within a week, which caps any ratio near 2 and compresses real differences.
