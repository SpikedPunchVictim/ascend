# EV-78: once the index arm has 5 sessions, do index sessions run `bd recall` more than full ones?

**Status**      The prediction was registered 2026-10-13 as `EV-69`'s P3 (*"the index arm's share is
                higher than the full arm's — direction only"*), before any handler was run. The
                segment-level analysis and its readiness condition were fixed the next day in
                `asc-09wx.17` (*"at least 20 segments from at least 5 sessions"*). Measured
                2026-10-23, when that condition first held.

**Question**    `EV-69` measured at the session unit and found the index arm too small to grade (3
                sessions). `segments.py` was written to use the finer unit — one beads SessionStart
                delivery up to the next one in the same session, split at a 60 s gap or an arm
                change — clustered by session. Does the direction P3 predicted survive when the
                unit is the delivery segment and the session is the cluster?

**The interval is corrected for the cluster, and that is the whole result.** `segments.py` prints a
Wilson interval at the raw segment count, which assumes its observations are independent. They are
not: one session contributes many segments. The pairs were exported (`/tmp/export_segments.py`,
the same segment construction, not a second analysis) and run through the package's own
`clusterDesign` and `wilson(k, n, 0.95, design)` (`packages/analysis/dist`, the estimator
`EV-71` and `EV-72` use). `smallGroup` is the package's own MIN_N verdict on the EFFECTIVE n.

## Decision

**P3's direction holds, and the segment analysis still cannot separate the arms.** 22.2% of index
segments ran `bd recall` against 0.5% of full segments; the raw intervals do not overlap, but the
cluster-corrected ones do — 3.6–68.9% against 0–65.6% — and BOTH arms are `smallGroup` on the
effective n (3.77 and 2.04). The bead's readiness condition (20 segments, 5 sessions) was met and is
not the bar that decides this: `rho` is 0.42–0.73 and the design effect 19–90, so 72 segments in 5
sessions buy less than 4 effective observations.

**`asc-09wx.17` closes with a negative result, not with a grade.** The regrade it asked for has been
run; the answer is that the index arm needs more SESSIONS, not more segments — the unit that is
short is the cluster, and no number of additional segments inside the existing five sessions can
fix it. `P5` (recalls name a stored key) holds on every counted call: 14 of 14 index recalls name a
key the index printed, 9 distinct keys.

**Caveat that outranks the numbers.** The arms are confounded with project and time: every index
session is this project after 2026-10-14, every full session is another project before 2026-09-27
(`EV-69`'s arm definition is the hook form, which is a project property here). This was known when
P3 was registered as direction-only, and nothing in this record changes it. A session-level design
inside one project is what would answer the causal question, and that is not what was run.

## Confidence

**High** in the counts: they are replayed rows, the exclusions match `EV-69`, and the segment
construction is `segments.py`'s own.

**Low** in the design-effect correction as a bound. `rho` estimated from 5 and 8 clusters is itself
noisy — the full arm's 0.73 is implausible as a population value and is a small-cluster artefact —
so the corrected interval says *"not separable"* and should not be read as a precise range.

**Certain** of the direction and of the secondary: no full-arm working session ran `bd recall` at
all, and every index recall named a key the index printed.
