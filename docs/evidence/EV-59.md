# EV-59: do n8n's three commit-time features predict a fix on nest?

**Status**      Measured 2026-10-13 for `asc-3gfl`. Predictions, the bar and the changes nest forces
                were in the bead's description before any blame trace was run; not sealed by
                sha256. The three `exploratory_` tables were added after the first run was read.

**Question**    `EV-58` learned and confirmed a rule inside one repository. Do the same features,
                with the same definitions and thresholds, predict the same outcome in a second?

## Method

`nest` at `c3bc75c973813969aa676793e20a7cba12a9daf5`, read-only (`git log`, `git diff`,
`git blame`). `EV-57`'s outcome and `EV-58`'s feature script, unchanged except for the arms: 1,111
`fix` commits since 2022 with 30 files or fewer traced by blame (1,142 traced rows); 1,524 eligible
commits.

Seen before predicting, counts only: the eligible count by year, 1,196 fix-typed commits, 5
agent-marked commits in 2026.

Fixed beforehand because nest forces it:

- The agent arm is dropped: under `MIN_N` (20).
- The rule was learned on n8n, so all 1,524 commits are the confirmation sample. **The bar** is
  `EV-58`'s confirmation bar: stratified risk ratio at least 1.3, with prevalence at least 5% and at
  least 20 exposed commits. Under that prevalence a feature is "cannot be graded".
- A split at 2024-01-01 (917 earlier, 607 later) is reported for stability and is not in the bar.
- Thresholds stay at n8n's values; size terciles are recomputed on nest.

## Predictions

P1 `large` reaches 1.3. P2 `hot_file` cannot be graded, prevalence under 5%. P3 `cross_package`
does not reach 1.3. P4 `no_test_alongside` lies between 0.7 and 1.3. P5 `first_touch` is below 1.0.
P6 the fixed rate rises with the count of the three features, 0 to 1 to 2-or-more. P7 the base
fixed rate is under 12%.

## Decision

**The n8n rule does not transfer as written. What transfers is size, and only when the threshold
is the repository's own.**

1. **Size replicates in direction and fails as an absolute number.** The fixed rate rises by
   tercile here too (6.4%, 10.1%, 14.1%), but nest's top tercile begins at 20 added lines and its
   90th percentile is 78, so "more than 200 lines" describes 3.4% of its commits. Exploratory:
   a top-decile threshold gives 1.95 (1.34 to 2.83) and a top-tercile one 1.62 (1.19 to 2.21).
2. **`cross_package` was the weakest clear on n8n and is nothing here.** Drop it.
3. **`hot_file` is not settled.** It clears overall, its exploratory interval is 0.97 to 2.28, and
   it is flat in one half of the history and 2.36 in the other.
4. **`no_test_alongside` disagrees between the two repositories.** That is not a result to build
   on in either direction. Exploratory: by change type the nest ratio is 2.05 for `feat`, 1.05 for
   `fix`, and every interval spans 1.
5. **The three-feature count, the form `EV-58` called usable, is not usable here**: four commits
   in five score zero.

The whole sample holds about 154 fixed commits, so every ratio in it is coarse. A third
repository of n8n's size would say more than this one could.

For the handler: nothing with a fixed line count or a fixed change count should be compiled. A
size rule has to be relative to the repository it runs in, which means a script reading local git
history beside the handler, since events carry no sizes.

## Confidence

**High** for the counts. **Medium** that size carries across repositories as a relative measure:
two repositories agree in direction, and the relative thresholds were chosen after reading the
result. **Low** for every other feature. Two repositories; blame's limits from `EV-57` apply.
