# EV-77: what is the commit-risk arm's size at the 489-unit ceiling?

**Status**      Measured 2026-10-22 for `asc-560h` and `asc-09wx.12`. No predictions were
                pre-registered; the question and the date were fixed by `EV-73` on 2026-10-15
                (*"a re-measurement on 2026-10-22 sees 483 of 489"*), and this is that run.

**Question**    `EV-71` and `EV-72` sized the commit-risk nudge at 8.2083 units per active day and
                70 active days at a 30% reduction, but 95 of the 489 source-bearing units still had
                their 7-day window open on 2026-10-15, so the figure was a floor. `EV-73` showed a
                refreshed commit map cannot move the ceiling and that the constraint is elapsed
                time. The question for 2026-10-22: with every window closed, what are the ceiling,
                the outcome, the design effect and the arm's days to power?

## Method

Read-only against the eight repositories and the transcripts. `NOW` is 1792713599
(2026-10-22T23:59:59Z), the last instant of the date `EV-73` named.

```
cost/numstat.py <repo> snap/at/<repo>.numstat.log at        # all eight maps, refreshed
cost/delta.py <gone-ev63-dir> . at at t4 all                # every source-bearing commit, one clock
cost/blame.py <repo> t4/D.at.<repo>.tsv t4/D.at.<repo>.out  # per commit: which earlier commit's lines it replaced
cost/remeasure.py out/join.jsonl . at out/rem-at-2026-10-22.json 1792713599 t4 <gone-ev63-dir>
spike/rework-design-effect/design.mjs out/rem-at-2026-10-22.json
cost/commits.mjs out/rem-at-2026-10-22.json
```

**The trace is FULL, not incremental, and that is a departure from `EV-73`'s method.** `EV-63`'s
`traced.tsv` — the only copy of the rows for the commits its `only_unfed` lists deliberately skip —
was in an OS temp tree and is gone (`asc-0qbw` predicted the reaping; `blame.py` was rebuilt for it
on 2026-10-18). A search of the repo, `.ascend-archived/`, `/tmp` and `/private/tmp` on 2026-10-22
found no `traced.tsv` and no `ev63` directory. An incremental `delta.py` would therefore have
re-listed and re-traced every commit that trace held, so the run took `delta.py`'s `all` mode
instead: every source-bearing commit in the refreshed map, traced under `%at`, which is also the
one-clock condition `EV-73` listed as unmeasured. Cost: 11,950 commits across eight repositories,
3,544.78 s of `blame` wall clock summed over the eight runs (59 minutes), 29,720 rework pairs
(against `EV-73`'s 21,820). The commit count is `wc -l t4/*.tsv` summed; `remeasure.py` reports
11,958 source-bearing commits in the map, and the untraced check it runs passes only because the
eight-commit difference is exempt as root commits, which have no parent to blame against.

## Decision

**The arm is registered at 65 active days for a 30% reduction and 25 at 50%, on 10.1875 units per
active day, baseline 0.78119, design effect 5.28545, effective N 92.518.** `asc-560h` closes; its
date has arrived and its number exists.

**`asc-09wx.12` installs the nudge now.** The build was never the sizing, and both halves are now in
hand: the holdout arm was exercised live on a foreign repo (`asc-hllr`), and this record supplies
the last number the installation waited on. The nudge is `handlers/commit-risk-nudge.yaml`, a
`say:` handler triggered on `tool.use.start` for Bash calls whose argv contains `commit`, installed
with `asc install-hook --holdout 0.3`.

## Confidence

**High** in the ceiling and the closure: 489 is unchanged under a map refresh, a full re-trace and
the later `NOW`, which is what `EV-73` predicted, and the closure schedule is empty rather than
imputed.

**High** in the rate, for the reason above: a missing `EV-63` trace is a measured 0.20 fall in this
exact number, and the number landed within 0.00054 of the registered value.

**Moderate** in the last two days of the design effect. `rho` moved 0.09588 -> 0.10062 and the
design effect 4.60581 -> 5.28545 as 95 units joined 24 sessions (29 clusters now, largest 63).
That is the direction `EV-71` warned about — more units in larger sessions can raise the design
effect while the raw rate is flat — and the effective N still rose because n grew faster. The arm's
days-to-power is therefore sensitive to the next accrual period's session sizes, not only to its
unit count.

**Not measured:** whether the nudge changes behaviour. That is the experiment, and this record only
sizes it. Nothing here says a delivered sentence reduces rework; it says what it would take to find
out at this unit rate.
