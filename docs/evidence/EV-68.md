# EV-68: do the commit signals hold in PostHog, and on commits an agent is marked on?

**Status** Predictions registered 2026-10-13 in the notes of `asc-09wx.6`, while the clone was
still downloading and before any PostHog commit had been read. Measured the same day.

**Question** Size, churn, heavy deletion, first touch and "no test alongside" were measured on nx
and n8n (EV-60, EV-66), which have few agent-marked commits. PostHog has thousands. Do the same
signals predict a later fix there, do they hold on the agent-marked commits taken alone, and are
agent-marked commits fixed more or less often than the rest?

## Method (fixed before measuring)

- **Repository:** `PostHog/posthog`, a bare single-branch clone with `--shallow-since=2021-11-01`
  and `--filter=blob:limit=60k`, read-only. Source blobs over the limit were fetched in bulk
  (43,785); images, snapshots and other non-source blobs were never downloaded. 2.2 GB on disk.
- **Outcome and fix definition** as EV-57: a commit is fixed when a `fix`-typed commit of 30 files
  or fewer rewrites one of its added source lines within 30 days. Eligible commits from 2022-01-01
  to 30 days before the last commit.
- **Not source:** EV-57's list plus `.ambr`, `__snapshots__/`, `.csv`, `.ipynb` and image and
  video types. **Test:** adds `test_*.py`, `*_test.py`, `conftest.py` and `tests/` directories.
  Generated files are not excluded.
- **Agent-marked:** EV-58's pattern on author name and co-author trailers.
- **Thresholds:** the 90th percentile of eligible commits 2022-2025, applied unchanged to 2026.
- **Statistic:** Mantel-Haenszel risk ratio with Greenland-Robins 95% interval; size stratified by
  commit type, the others by size decile and type.
- **Bar** (EV-60's): ratio at least 1.3 on all commits with 5% prevalence and 20 exposed, and at
  least 1.3 in 2026.

### Differences from EV-57's tracer, decided while running it

- The diff is limited to source paths by pathspec, and the blame walk stops 31 days before the fix
  (`--since`); a line older than that lands on a boundary commit, which is dropped. A 30-day
  outcome needs nothing older.
- **The first full run was discarded.** In the partial clone, a blame that reaches a file's
  creation runs rename detection, meets a blob that was never downloaded, scans every pack (about
  six minutes, measured on one blame: `371.019265` s to `exit code:128`) and dies with
  `fatal: could not fetch ... from promisor remote`. The tracer read only standard output, so the
  file's lines were dropped without a message. The second tracer runs git through a scratch git
  directory that borrows the clone's objects and has no promisor remote; the same blame fails in
  about a second naming the blob, the blob is fetched, and the blame is retried. Failures are
  written to a file. Result: 66 blobs fetched, 1 failure (a path containing a space in `.run/`),
  the whole list in 1,739 s. On the 7,500 fixes the first run had completed, the second run has
  every row of the first and 38 more (14,019 against 14,057).

## Predictions

- **P1.** The base fixed rate is between 10% and 20%.
- **P2.** `size_top_decile` clears the bar.
- **P3.** In the agent-marked 2026 arm `size_top_decile` is at least 1.3 with the interval above 1.
- **P4.** Heavy deletion (top tenth by removed lines) is at least 1.3 with the interval above 1.
- **P5.** `churn_top_decile` is at least 1.3 before, and below 1.3 after, later activity (EV-66's
  definition) is held fixed.
- **P6.** `first_touch` is below 1.0.
- **P7.** `no_test_alongside` lies between 0.7 and 1.3.
- **P8.** In 2026, agent-marked against the rest, stratified by size decile and type, lies between
  0.85 and 1.15.
- **P9.** A larger share of agent-marked 2026 commits than of the rest is in the size top decile.

## Decision

1. **Size is the signal, in a third public repository and on agent-marked commits.** The ratio is
   about 2.1 in every arm, and 1.87 with later activity held fixed. Nothing here changes the plan
   to build the size check first.
2. **Heavy deletion clears 1.3 overall and with later activity held fixed (1.32),** as in nx and
   n8n, but not in the agent-marked arm (1.24) and not on our own repositories (EV-67). The
   recorder stores removed lines; no flag is built on them yet.
3. **Churn stays unbuilt.** It is under the bar before any control and indistinguishable from 1
   once later activity is held fixed, the same shape as n8n in EV-66.
4. **Agent-marked commits are larger and are fixed more often even at equal size and type** (30.7%
   against 22.3%; 1.23 after stratifying). This is the first arm large enough to say so. It is an
   association between a mark and an outcome, not a comparison of agents with people.
5. **The base rate is about double nx and n8n.** The 30-file fix rule and the outcome are the same,
   so the ratio comparisons stand, but rates are not comparable across repositories.

## Confidence

**High** for size and **medium** for heavy deletion and churn within this repository: the arms hold
thousands of exposed commits. **Low** for what the agent gap means. A mark shows that an agent was
credited, and unmarked 2026 commits may also be agent-written, which would shrink the measured gap;
marked commits may also be a different kind of work, which size decile and type do not capture.
The clone is shallow and partial, the blame walk is capped at 31 days, generated files such as
`frontend/snapshots.yml` are counted as source, and one blame failed and is missing from the trace.
